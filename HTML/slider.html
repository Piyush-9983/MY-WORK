OCTOPUS

Octopuses are intelligent marine animals known for their soft bodies, eight limbs, and remarkable ability to camouflage. They use their agility and ink defense to escape predators.

SHARK

Sharks are a group of elasmobranch fish characterized by a cartilaginous skeleton. They have five to seven gill slits and powerful senses for hunting in the deep sea.

DOLPHIN

Dolphins are highly social and intelligent mammals, often seen swimming in pods. They communicate using clicks and whistles and are known for their playful behavior.
